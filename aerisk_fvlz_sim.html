Safeguarded dispatch in the Fuerteventura‑Lanzarote system

Reproduces in the browser the hourly commitment and dispatch model described in the article: two nodes joined by the 132 kV link, sixteen thermal units, N‑1 criterion with deliverability, and four reserve allocation policies. Change the parameters and simulate the 8,760 hours again.

Risk ribbon

Each vertical strip is one hour of the year, ordered from January to December. Height encodes the residual risk ρt of the automatic recommendation; color, whether the safeguard was active. The horizontal line marks the threshold in force.

The ribbon shows why the safeguard can be neither permanent nor uniform: risk concentrates in episodes, and the threshold decides how much of that profile turns into effective conservatism.

Model equivalent to that of the aerisk_fvlz package (version 2.0). Thermal fleet parameters, variable costs, and emission factors derived from fuel price, lower heating value, net efficiency, and IPCC default factors (2006). Calibration magnitudes: Red Eléctrica, Comité Técnico de Seguimiento de la Operación del Sistema Eléctrico de Canarias, enero–diciembre 2025 (March 2026). This in-browser implementation uses double-precision arithmetic and the same decision rules, but a pseudorandom generator different from numpy's: the values do not match those of the campaign digit by digit; the published figures are those of the article.
